4. Wound Haven SMS Communication Program

Wound Haven offers an optional SMS communication program for individuals who agree to receive text messages regarding their interactions with Wound Haven.

Depending on the type of request and the recipient's consent, messages may include:

  • Referral inquiry follow-ups
  • Appointment scheduling information
  • Administrative coordination
  • Provider referral updates
  • Appropriate care coordination communications

The current SMS program is intended for informational and administrative communications, not third-party marketing or promotional messaging.

5. How SMS Consent Is Obtained

Individuals may opt in through a designated SMS consent checkbox on applicable Wound Haven forms or another approved consent process.

The SMS checkbox is optional and should not be preselected.

Submitting a contact form, providing a phone number, or submitting a patient referral does not automatically enroll someone in optional SMS messaging.

Healthcare providers submitting information about a patient do not automatically provide SMS consent on that patient's behalf.

Consent to receive text messages is not required to submit a referral or receive Wound Haven services.

6. Message Frequency and Charges

Message and data rates may apply.

Wound Haven is not responsible for charges imposed by your mobile carrier.

7. SMS Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of receiving Wound Haven text messages at any time by replying:

STOP

After opting out, you will no longer receive optional SMS messages from the applicable messaging program unless you provide new consent, except for messages permitted or required to confirm or process your opt-out.

Withdrawing SMS consent will not prevent you from submitting referrals or requesting services.

Other permitted communication methods may remain available.
