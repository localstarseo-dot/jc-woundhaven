Who we are

A wound-care focus.
A patient-centered model.

Wound Haven is focused on helping patients who need specialized wound-care support in the environments where they live or receive care.

Rather than relying on a traditional office-based model, our team provides care in homes and supported healthcare environments when appropriate.

Our clinical approach may include wound assessment, treatment, monitoring, education, and coordination based on the patient’s wound and care needs.

Our care model

Mobile wound care.
Closer to the patient.

Wound Haven’s mobile model is designed to bring appropriate wound-care services closer to patients in Austin, Houston, San Antonio, College Station, Corpus Christi, Beaumont, and surrounding areas.

What does Wound Haven do?

Wound Haven provides advanced wound-care services for appropriate patients in homes and supported care environments.

Is Wound Haven an in-office wound-care clinic?

No. Wound Haven’s current model is focused on mobile and bedside wound care rather than traditional in-office visits.

Where can care be provided?

Care may be provided in homes, skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, rehabilitation facilities, and LTAC settings.

What types of wounds does Wound Haven support?

Wound Haven supports care for diabetic wounds, pressure injuries, venous wounds, arterial wounds, lymphedema-related wounds, burns and skin-related wounds, non-healing wounds, and other complex wounds.

Explore Wounds We Treat →
What wound-care services may be available?

Services may include wound assessment, debridement, compression therapy, vascular assessment, diagnostic support, negative pressure wound therapy, advanced wound-care options, education, and ongoing wound management where clinically appropriate.

Yes. Austin is one of Wound Haven’s primary service areas, with mobile care also available in our other supported Texas communities.
